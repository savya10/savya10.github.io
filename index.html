About

I build machine-learning models that accelerate the search for new semiconductor materials, specifically to predict how defects and dopants behave across different charge states. I've just submitted my PhD in Computational Materials Science, a joint programme between UCL (Prof. David Scanlon) and A*STAR Singapore (Dr Kedar Hippalgaonkar), funded by the ARAP Research Scholarship.

My thesis, Accelerating materials design of defect-implemented semiconductors with machine learning, centres on building structure-property relationships using charge-aware MLIPs for point defect-implemented materials. During my time in Singapore I also worked on generative modelling approaches for disordered crystal structures (Dis-GEN) and predictive materials informatics (DFTPilot). Before that I completed my MSci in Chemistry at Imperial College London, where I built PyTASER in Aron Walsh's group.

In my downtime, I'm an avid martial artist (Taekwondo black belt and England international competitor), a former national-level chess player, and a collector of languages (5 so far!).

Projects

Featured

DefectMLIP

Trained charge-aware machine learning interatomic potentials to predict defect energetics and formation energies, using newly-released architectures (QET, MACE-LES, MACE-QEq,...). Assessed on a dataset of over 100k internally calculated materials on point defect simulations calculated using hybrid-DFT (HSE06, PBE0) calculations.

Accelerated traditional first principles workflow by up to 220x, with a maximum dopability error less than 0.23 eV. Tested for 6 different systems with ongoing further tests for high-throughput application. Preparing preprint for release in October 2026.

MLIPsDefectsCharge-AwareHybrid-DFTHigh-throughput

Defect GNNs

Constructed 4 novel GNN-based structural representations for property prediction of point defects in semiconductors. Used cross-validation methods to test increasing blindness in prediction, achieving a best-case MAE of 0.45 eV across 22 systems. Previously untested for a dataset this universal/diverse.

pytorch · GNNs · dataset

PyTASER

Python package to simulate transient absorption spectroscopy of crystals from first principles. I'm the lead developer and maintainer.

python · DFT · spectroscopy · JOSS 2024

DFTPilot

An LLM assistant that turns experimental set-ups into DFT simulation inputs, using RAG and a FastAPI front end. I led a team of 6; it won the Visionary Award at the 2025 LLM Hackathon (120 projects).

LLMs · RAG · FastAPI

Dis-GEN

A Wyckoff-based generator for disordered crystalline structures. Validated against 10+ systems pulled from the ICSD.

VAE · disorder · ICSD · arxiv

Nyāya

A multi-agent scientific hypothesis engine for generating and evaluating research hypotheses using a Nyāya reasoning framework. Proposed 100 novel systems from a causal map built using the ICSD, with lightweight MLIP validation tests.

LLMs · multi-agent · ICSD
